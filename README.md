# CivicOS + GramRakshak

AI-Powered Civic Issue Management & Smart Governance Platform

## Problem

Citizens often face delays in reporting and resolving civic issues.
Government departments also lack centralized visibility and intelligent
prioritization of complaints.

## Solution

CivicOS + GramRakshak provides a unified platform for:

Citizen Reporting
        ↓
Smart Department Routing
        ↓
Priority & SLA Calculation
        ↓
Worker Assignment
        ↓
Field Resolution
        ↓
Before/After Proof
        ↓
Citizen Tracking
        ↓
Government Analytics

## Key Features

- Citizen complaint reporting
- Emergency SOS
- Location and photo evidence
- Smart department routing
- Automatic priority calculation
- SLA management
- Automatic escalation
- Worker assignment
- Worker dashboard
- Civic Intelligence Map

- Department performance analytics

## Hackathon Demo Flow

Citizen reports an issue
→ CivicOS identifies the department
→ Priority and SLA are calculated
→ Department assigns a worker
→ Worker updates the task
→ Resolution proof is uploaded
→ Citizen tracks the complaint
→ Government Command Center displays analytics
