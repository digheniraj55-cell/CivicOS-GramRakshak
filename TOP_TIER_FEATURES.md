# CivicOS — Top-Tier Hackathon Feature Set

## Operational foundation
- Citizen complaint portal with photo + exact location evidence.
- SOS workflow.
- Complaint ID recovery using citizen name + phone.
- Complaint tracking, timeline, browser notifications and optional email.
- Smart department routing with optional AI classification and deterministic fallback.
- Worker assignment, one-active-task-per-team database enforcement and automatic queue pull.
- SLA deadlines + automatic escalation.
- Before/after evidence, feedback/upvotes, department dashboards, ward analytics, public transparency and Government Command Center.

## Municipal Intelligence layer

### 1. Civic Memory + Causal Graph
Connects recurring nearby issues and cross-department symptoms, then surfaces a probable root-cause clue with confidence and a field-inspection recommendation.

### 2. Civic Blind-Spot Radar
Separates observed reporting from estimated operational stress. Silent-zone results trigger inspection recommendations; they do not claim a failure as fact.

### 3. Civic Risk Forecast
Produces explainable ward-level 7-day risk from recurrence, priority, emergency and SLA stress.

### 4. Digital Twin Lite / Cost of Waiting
Compares “act now” with “wait N days,” including estimated extra cost, danger growth and downstream cascade chain.

### 5. Public Value Optimizer
A constrained, explainable hackathon optimizer that selects high-value interventions under budget, workforce, vehicle and time limits. It reports estimated citizens benefited, incidents prevented and delay cost avoided.

### 6. Civic Impact & Service Equity
Compares service outcomes by ward using unresolved burden, case age, recurrence and SLA breaches. It deliberately avoids demographic/protected-attribute inference.

### 7. Chronic Failure + Civic Debt
Detects resolve→recur cycles and estimates repeat-repair burden so the authority can switch from surface repair to root-cause correction.

### 8. Proof-of-Resolution Engine
Requires after-work evidence before resolution. It checks before/after availability, image quality, visual-change signal and citizen confirmation. Visual comparison is treated as evidence support—not autonomous truth.

### 9. Complaint → Policy Intelligence
Turns stable recurring patterns into explainable structural recommendations.

### 10. Civic Cascade Engine
Shows how one unresolved issue may propagate into additional public impact and estimates a prototype cascade risk.

### 11. Civic Health Index
Measures ward infrastructure/service health rather than only complaint closure counts.

## Handwritten hackathon concepts integrated
- **How expensive/dangerous this becomes if we wait** → Digital Twin Lite + delay liability.
- **Cross Department Sweep** → persistent coordinated sweep missions across connected complaints.
- **One Trip, Multiple Problems** → nearby inspection/route batching while preserving individual task assignment rules.
- **Civic Debt** → explicit debt score + projected delay liability.
- **Never spend the last resource** → Reserve Capacity Guard in automatic and manual assignment.
- **During Disaster** → Disaster Operations Mode protects additional emergency response capacity.

## Important model boundary
The intelligence layer is intentionally decision support. It ranks, explains and recommends inspection/action. It does not autonomously make policy, claim causation as certainty, infer protected demographics, or treat image difference as proof by itself.
