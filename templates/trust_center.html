How it works

Verification without pretending the algorithm knows everything

1Authority source registryGovernment corrections and advisories are published with source, time and verdict.
2Explainable text matchingForwarded wording is matched locally against known bulletins—even if formatting changed.
3Human review for uncertaintyUnknown claims go to a Trust Queue instead of being guessed as true or false.
4Coordinated-abuse detectionNear-identical complaints across accounts or wards are risk-scored and can be quarantined from public feeds.
