GramRakshak + CivicOS

One connected civic operations layer

Citizen reporting stays simple while departments gain controlled assignment, SLA accountability, mapping and performance intelligence.

01

Smart Department Routing

Complaint text and category route issues to the correct department with a reliable local fallback.

02

Worker Assignment Control

Each field team can hold only one unresolved task. New work remains prioritized in a queue until a team is free.

03

SLA + Escalation

Every complaint receives a service deadline and overdue work escalates automatically.

04

Citizen Update Loop

Complaint recovery, stored updates, browser alerts and optional email keep citizens informed.

Civic Intelligence

From reacting to complaints → preventing repeat failures

CivicOS uses the operational history already created by citizens and field teams to surface explainable prevention signals for authorities.

01

Connect Root Causes

Nearby cross-department symptoms become probable causal clusters instead of isolated tickets.

02

Forecast Cost & Risk

Compare act-now versus wait scenarios, civic debt, cascade risk and ward health.

03

Protect Scarce Capacity

Optimize public value, coordinate sweeps and keep emergency reserve teams available.

04

Verify & Learn

Before/after proof and citizen confirmation feed Civic Memory so recurring failures become visible.
